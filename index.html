line 2 · work

Work

I run paid ads for tree service businesses in Florida. Google and Meta campaigns that bring in estimate requests, plus the follow-up so those requests turn into booked jobs. I work with a small number of owners across the state.

How it works

  1. Quick call. We talk through your service area, your crews, and what you're doing now for leads.
  2. Campaigns go live. I build the ads and landing pages and run them in your area. You approve the budget before anything spends.
  3. Estimates come in. Homeowner requests come to you by call and text. I follow up on the ones that don't answer so they don't go cold.

What I do

  • Google Search and Local Services ads for tree removal, trimming, and stump grinding
  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram) campaigns for storm and seasonal work
  • Landing pages built for estimate requests
  • Lead follow-up by text and call, so no request sits unanswered
  • Weekly reporting on spend, requests, and booked estimates
