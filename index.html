About me

I'm Zachary Spencer. I'm an accountancy student at the University of South Florida and the founder of Zenith Co., an agency that runs paid ads for Florida tree service companies. I'm based in Tampa, and most people call me Zac.

I started Zenith Co. by building websites for tree companies. The first was for A Tree Surgeons Enterprise in Tampa, followed by King's Tree Service in Palm Bay and Andy's PGM in Sarasota. Building those sites, I kept seeing the same thing: the website was rarely what lost the job. A homeowner called while the crew was up a tree, nobody called back for hours, and the work went to whoever answered first.

So the agency now does one thing. We run Google and Meta ads for established tree companies, text and call every lead in the company's name, and book the estimate on the owner's calendar. Owners pay for each estimate where the homeowner was actually there, not a monthly retainer.

I work with one company per service area and take every call myself. The rest of the week I'm in class, or writing down what I learn from this work. There is a longer version here.

How it works

1. Quick callWe talk through your service area, your crews, and what you're doing now for leads.
2. Campaigns go liveI build the ads and landing pages and run them in your area. You approve the budget before anything spends.
3. Estimates come inHomeowner requests come to you by call and text. I follow up on the ones that don't answer so they don't go cold.

What I do

  • Google Search and Local Services ads for tree removal, trimming, and stump grinding
  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram) campaigns for storm and seasonal work
  • Landing pages built for estimate requests
  • Lead follow-up by text and call, so no request sits unanswered
  • Weekly reporting on spend, requests, and booked estimates

This work runs through Zenith Co., the agency I founded. The terms, pricing and client sites are at zenithcomarketing.com.
