About Zachary Spencer

I'm Zachary Spencer, the founder of Zenith Co. and an accountancy student at the University of South Florida. I'm based in Tampa, Florida. Most people call me Zac.

Zenith Co. runs paid ads for established tree service companies in Florida and turns the leads into booked estimates on the owner's calendar. I work with that one trade, and with one company per service area.

What I do

A tree company that works with Zenith Co. pays three things: its ad spend, straight to the ad platforms on its own card; a one-time setup fee; and a flat fee for each estimate where the homeowner was there to walk the job. There is no monthly retainer and no long-term contract. I wrote about why I bill per shown estimate, including where that model does not fit.

I take every call with a company that is considering working with us, and I'm the one responsible for the ads after they sign. The full terms are on the Zenith Co. pricing page.

How I got here

I started by building websites for tree companies. A Tree Surgeons Enterprise in Tampa was my first client. After that came King's Tree Service in Palm Bay, whose Google Ads I also run, and Andy's PGM in Sarasota, a company that had been doing tree work since 1988 without a website of its own. All three sites are live, and the Zenith Co. work page describes each build.

Building them, I kept running into the same thing. The website was rarely what lost the job. A homeowner called while the crew was up a tree, nobody called back for hours, and the homeowner hired whoever answered first. That is why the agency's main service is built around answering fast, and why I only get paid when the estimate actually happens.

School

I study accountancy at the University of South Florida. It is more useful in this work than it sounds. Before a tree company owner cares about an ad, they want to know what an estimate costs them and what it is worth, and that is a unit-cost question before it is a marketing one.

I'm also studying for the LSAT.
