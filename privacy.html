1. Text messaging (SMS) policy

When you provide your mobile number through the chat widget on this Site and check the consent box, you agree to receive text messages from me related to your inquiry, including replies to your questions and appointment coordination. If you separately check the promotional consent box, you also agree to receive occasional messages about my services and offers.

  • Opt out: reply STOP to any message at any time.

Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent will not be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except that phone numbers may be processed by the messaging platform I use (LeadConnector) solely to deliver the messages you have consented to receive.
